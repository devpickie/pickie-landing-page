Welcome to Pickie

Pickie is built to help people find compatible sports partners, build meaningful connections, and enjoy playing together.

Today, Pickie focuses on pickleball. As we grow, we may expand to support more sports and communities. No matter the sport, our mission remains the same:

Create a safe, respectful, and welcoming environment where people can connect through sport.

These Community Standards explain the behavior we expect from everyone using Pickie. By using Pickie, you agree to follow these standards.

1. Treat Everyone with Respect

Respect is the foundation of every great community.

Every player should feel welcome, regardless of:

  • Skill level
  • Experience
  • Age
  • Gender
  • Nationality
  • Language
  • Background
  • Playing style

We encourage positive communication, good sportsmanship, and inclusive behavior both online and on the court.

We do not tolerate:

  • Harassment or bullying
  • Hate speech
  • Threats or intimidation
  • Discrimination
  • Personal attacks
  • Abusive or offensive behavior

2. Be Honest

Great matches start with accurate information.

Please:

  • Use your real identity.
  • Upload photos that represent you.
  • Provide accurate information about yourself.
  • Keep your profile information updated.
  • Represent your skill level honestly.

Do not:

  • Create fake accounts.
  • Impersonate another person.
  • Misrepresent your playing ability.
  • Use bots, automated accounts, or fake activity.

3. Use Pickie for Genuine Sporting Connections

Pickie exists to help people connect through sport.

Whether you are looking for a casual game, training partner, tournament partner, or new friends, Pickie is designed for genuine sporting connections.

Do not use Pickie to:

  • Send spam or unwanted promotions.
  • Scam or defraud other users.
  • Request money from other users.
  • Advertise unrelated products or services without permission.
  • Conduct illegal activities.
  • Misuse the platform for purposes unrelated to sports and community building.

4. Respect Personal Boundaries

Everyone has different comfort levels and expectations.

Respect other people’s choices and boundaries.

Do not:

  • Send repeated unwanted messages.
  • Pressure someone to meet.
  • Make unwanted romantic or sexual advances.
  • Send sexually explicit content.
  • Continue contacting someone after they have declined or blocked you.

Good connections are built on mutual respect.

5. Protect Privacy and Safety

Your safety and the safety of other players matter.

Please:

  • Meet other players in public locations whenever possible.
  • Respect other people’s privacy.
  • Be thoughtful before sharing personal information.
  • Use good judgment when meeting someone for the first time.

Do not:

  • Share another user’s personal information without permission.
  • Attempt to track, stalk, or intimidate another user.
  • Use Pickie to facilitate harmful or illegal activities.

6. Practice Good Sportsmanship

Every sport is competitive, but respect always comes first.

Whether you are playing casually or competing at a high level, we encourage everyone to:

  • Play fairly.
  • Respect teammates, partners, opponents, and officials.
  • Encourage new players.
  • Celebrate great games.
  • Accept wins and losses respectfully.
  • Help create a positive sporting environment.

Great chemistry is not only about winning — it is about enjoying the game together.

7. Content That Is Not Allowed

To keep Pickie safe and welcoming, users may not share or promote:

  • Harassment or abusive content
  • Hate speech or discriminatory content
  • Threats of violence
  • Sexually explicit content
  • Illegal activities
  • Fraud or scams
  • Fake identities
  • Spam
  • Unsolicited advertising
  • Content promoting gambling, drugs, or criminal activity
  • Content intended to deceive, exploit, or harm others

8. Reporting and Enforcement

If you see behavior that violates these Community Standards, you can:

  • Report a user
  • Block a user
  • Contact Pickie support

Our team reviews reports and may take actions including:

  • Warning the user
  • Removing content
  • Restricting account features
  • Temporarily suspending an account
  • Permanently removing an account

We may take action without prior notice when necessary to protect the Pickie community.

Our goal is to maintain a safe, fair, and enjoyable experience for everyone.

9. Building the Future of Sports Communities

Every swipe, match, conversation, and game shapes the community we are building together.

Pickie is created for people who love sports — from beginners discovering a new activity to experienced players looking for better partners.

Together, we can build a global sports community where people connect, compete, improve, and enjoy the games they love.

Thank you for being part of Pickie.
