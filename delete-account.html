Delete Your Pickie Account

At Pickie, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your account and personal data. You can permanently delete your Pickie account at any time directly from within the app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. 1Open the Pickie app.
  2. 2Go to Profile.
  3. 3Tap Account.
  4. 4Select Security.
  5. 5Scroll to Danger Zone.
  6. 6Tap Delete Account.
  7. 7Confirm your deletion request.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the personal data associated with your account, including:

  • Profile information
  • Email address
  • Profile photos
  • Bio
  • Playing preferences
  • Matches
  • Account settings

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained for a limited period where required by applicable laws or for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Payment and transaction records
  • Fraud prevention and security logs
  • Information required to comply with legal obligations

Retained information is stored only for as long as necessary.
